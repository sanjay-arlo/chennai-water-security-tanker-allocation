# Business Case

## Problem
Chennai water-supply decisions can be distorted by citywide averages. The business need is to identify where supply stress is concentrated and prioritise scarce tanker, conservation and recharge resources.

## Proposed analytical intervention
Build a governed decision-support product connecting operational indicators, KPI definitions, business rules, scenario assumptions and an action-oriented dashboard.

## Value hypothesis
Decision-makers can allocate scarce resources against measurable exposure instead of relying on a single headline storage figure.

## Success measures
- Priority units identified in under 2 minutes.
- Every KPI has a documented formula and source field.
- High-exposure units receive an explicit action recommendation.
- Data-quality failures are detected before dashboard publication.

## Guardrails
The synthetic portfolio dataset and the 2026 external reference snapshot are kept separate. External figures are contextual anchors and must not substitute for governed operational source systems.
